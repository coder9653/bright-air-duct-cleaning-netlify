Our Story

Rooted in Expertise, Focused on Results

Bright Air Duct Cleaning began operations in 2008 with a clear purpose: to give Tampa Bay property owners access to dependable, professional ventilation services. What started as a residential air duct cleaning company has since expanded into a full-service ventilation provider covering air ducts, dryer vents, and chimney systems.

Operating from our Tampa headquarters at 12610 Henderson Rd #2A, our NADCA-certified technicians serve a broad area that includes Tampa, Clearwater, St. Petersburg, Brandon, and many neighboring communities throughout the region.

Our guiding principle has remained unchanged from day one: inspect with care, recommend with honesty, and execute with professionalism. That consistency has earned us the confidence of more than 5,000 area homeowners, along with a growing portfolio of commercial clients who rely on us for their ongoing ventilation needs.

Bright Air Duct Cleaning continues to serve both residential and commercial properties with the personalized attention that has been the hallmark of our work since the beginning. Our NADCA certification reflects the ongoing training and accountability our technicians bring to every single project.

Our Philosophy

How We Approach Every Project

Bright Air Duct Cleaning never cuts corners. Each service appointment begins with a proper inspection because truly understanding the system is the only path to a sound recommendation. Our technicians are trained to assess conditions thoroughly, not simply clean on autopilot, and that difference is significant.

We have earned our reputation by doing the work right and pricing it fairly. Property owners across Tampa Bay who have used our services once tend to come back because our work is dependable. No high-pressure sales tactics, no unnecessary add-ons, just solid professional service supported by NADCA-certified training.
