Local Conditions

The Same Climate, Different Buildings

Dade City's long, humid, rainy summers affect homes and commercial buildings alike. Moisture can build up in ducts and encourage mold and microbial growth, and pollen and fine particles add to indoor contamination.

The effect differs by building. In homes, it may mean odors and dust. In restaurants or medical settings, where hygiene and airflow matter to many people at once, it can be a more pressing concern.

Climate Impacts by Property

Moisture and microbial growth
Pollen and fine particles
Hot attic duct runs
Aging systems in older buildings
