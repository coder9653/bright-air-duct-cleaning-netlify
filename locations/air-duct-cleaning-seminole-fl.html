Local Conditions

Coastal Humidity in Homes and Businesses

Condensation forms when warm, humid air meets cold ducts, and damp duct interiors can grow mold and mildew. Salt air from Boca Ciega Bay and a long pollen season add more buildup.

Commercial systems, with longer hours and heavier daily activity, collect contaminants even faster than residential ones.

Homes

For Seminole Homeowners

Many Seminole homes were built decades ago and still rely on their original ducts. Those systems can hold years of dust and debris, and aging flex duct and joints may be leaking or collapsing.

Common residential signs include uneven cooling, visible dust around vents, musty air, and energy bills that climb without explanation. A camera inspection shows whether the ducts are behind those symptoms.

Residential cleaning uses a truck-mounted HEPA vacuum and rotating brushes, and many jobs are finished the same day. Sanitizing or a UV light can follow where moisture has encouraged growth.
