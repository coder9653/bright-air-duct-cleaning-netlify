Inspection

Assessments for Homes and Commercial Buildings

For homes, technicians walk through and inspect the duct system for buildup, mold, disconnected joints, and blockages. For commercial buildings, they assess the full duct system before recommending work.

Assessment Includes

  • Full duct system review
  • HD camera inspection
  • CFM airflow levels
  • Plenum and joint seals
  • Mold and moisture
  • Buildup and blockages

High-definition video cameras and CFM airflow reports are used in both settings to show what is happening inside the ducts.

Local Conditions

Humidity Affects Homes and Businesses Alike

Land O' Lakes' warm, humid climate and its many lakes and wetlands contribute moisture and airborne spores. Year-round AC use pulls that material into the ducts of homes and businesses.

In commercial buildings, humidity, foot traffic, and long operating hours combine, so ductwork collects contaminants quickly.

Homes

What Land O' Lakes Homeowners Should Know

Most homes in Land O' Lakes run their air conditioning through much of the year, so ducts steadily collect dust, mites, allergens, and pet hair. Humidity can add mold to the mix.

Common residential signs include debris around vents, rooms that feel airless or more humid than others, stale air when the AC starts, and more sneezing indoors. Renovations are another frequent trigger, since construction dust settles in ducts quickly.

Families with pets often choose sanitizing after cleaning, and households that deal with persistent mold may consider a UV light in the air handler.

Businesses

What Land O' Lakes Businesses Should Know

Running a business in Land O' Lakes means the AC rarely rests. Offices and retail spaces see steady traffic and long hours, so commercial ductwork can collect contaminants faster than a home system.

The team begins every commercial job by assessing the building's entire duct system, then uses specialized equipment to clear what is restricting airflow. Most commercial jobs are completed the same day, which helps limit disruption.

If employees have been complaining about stuffy air, or if a build-out has just finished, an assessment is a practical first step.
